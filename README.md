**Author:** [sesasa](https://github.com/sesasa)

## In short

1. **Hidden agency.** Civilization's feedback and self-organization hide the people who think, model and design. The body's self-organization may likewise hide microscopic, possibly quantum-level agents that model and engineer biological systems.
2. **Recursive scaling.** Evolution may be those agents' building work: they design systems, automate what works and move on to new problems. Consciousness comes late, as a way for an already intelligent system to scale beyond its body. Civilization, with AI as its emerging brain, may be the next level of the same pattern.

Parts of these ideas may already exist in philosophy, biology or astrobiology. This repo records this line of reasoning and its dates; it does not claim to be the first.
